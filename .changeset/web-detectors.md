Add web framework detection. The `web` detector recognizes Next.js, Nuxt, Astro, SvelteKit, Remix, Angular, SolidStart, Gatsby, Eleventy, Vite, webpack and Parcel from the project's dependency list, plus `detectWeb` to compose the result and `isStaticWebFramework` to tell static-output frameworks apart.
